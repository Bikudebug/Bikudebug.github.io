⚠️ LUGGAGE ALERT — read before the JR counter

Carrying 141 cm Magnum bag + 164.5 cm HRX bag.

ACTION REQUIRED: the HRX bag is over 160 cm, so at the KIX JR Ticket Office you MUST explicitly ask for a “Seat with an Oversized Baggage Area” on the Shinkansen. Boarding without that reservation = 1,000 JPY penalty on the train.

Also: student fare = 40 kg check-in + 7 kg cabin, but only if you show passport, student visa and the university acceptance letter at HYD check-in.
