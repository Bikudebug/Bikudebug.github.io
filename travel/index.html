⚠️ LUGGAGE ALERT — read before the JR counter

Carrying 141 cm Magnum bag + 164.5 cm HRX bag.

ACTION REQUIRED: the HRX bag is over 160 cm, so at the KIX JR Ticket Office you MUST explicitly ask for a “Seat with an Oversized Baggage Area” on the Shinkansen. Boarding without that reservation = 1,000 JPY penalty on the train.

Also: student fare = 40 kg check-in + 7 kg cabin, but only if you show passport, student visa and the university acceptance letter at HYD check-in.

🆘 Emergency contact & updates

Type the details once and tap Save — they are kept only on this phone, never on the internet. Each send button opens WhatsApp or your mail app with the whole message already written; you just press Send. (A webpage can’t send messages silently in the background — this one-tap way is the safe, reliable alternative.)

The status message includes where you are right now, the full next step, and the link to this page — so your contact can watch the same moving map (tell them the page password yourself, don’t write it in the message). Reminders ring on this phone 45 min before each step and again at step time, with the complete instructions — they work while the app is open, so keep it open (🔆 Keep awake) during the journey.

🧭 Journey timeline

Tap a step to mark it done. Progress is saved on this phone.

    🚇 Nagoya Station → dorm by subway

    Fare 430–470 JPY either way. Tap 🚇 Metro on the map to see both routes drawn in the real line colours. (With the two big bags on move-in day, the taxi below is easier — the subway is your everyday route.)

    RED Plan A — Sakura-dōri Line

    1. At Nagoya Station find the Sakura-dōri Line (red). Board the train toward Tokushige (徳重).
    2. Ride ~5 min, get off at Hisaya-ōdōri (久屋大通).
    3. Change to the Meijō Line (purple) — clockwise (右回り), i.e. for Nagoya Dome-mae Yada.
    4. Ride ~15 min, get off at Nagoya Dome-mae Yada (ナゴヤドーム前矢田).

    YELLOW Plan B — Higashiyama Line

    1. At Nagoya Station find the Higashiyama Line (yellow). Board and ride ~5 min.
    2. Get off at Sakae (栄).
    3. Change to the Meijō Line (purple) — clockwise (右回り), i.e. for Nagoya Dome-mae Yada.
    4. Ride ~12 min, get off at Nagoya Dome-mae Yada (ナゴヤドーム前矢田).

    WALK Station → International Residence Daiko (~3 min)

    1. Take the elevator up to Entrance 1 (1番出口).
    2. Turn right at the corner just outside the exit.
    3. Walk straight ~3 min — International Residence Daiko is on your right.

    🗂️ Document vault (stored only on this phone)

    Files are saved in this browser’s private storage. They are never uploaded anywhere.

    Phone ↔ laptop sync: tap Backup on the device that has the files, then have the encrypted file published to the site (ask Claude on the laptop). Every device that opens the app will then offer to load it — password required. Or move it by hand: send the file to yourself and tap Restore. Either way the file is useless without the password.

    🚄 My train tickets — live status

    When you buy a ticket, add it here — the card then shows a live countdown → board soon → en route → arrived, updating by itself. All times are Japan time (JST). Saved only on this device. For live delays and platform numbers use the Navitime link in Quick links.
