⚠️ LUGGAGE ALERT — read before the JR counter

Carrying 141 cm Magnum bag + 164.5 cm HRX bag.

ACTION REQUIRED: the HRX bag is over 160 cm, so at the KIX JR Ticket Office you MUST explicitly ask for a “Seat with an Oversized Baggage Area” on the Shinkansen. Boarding without that reservation = 1,000 JPY penalty on the train.

Also: student fare = 40 kg check-in + 7 kg cabin, but only if you show passport, student visa and the university acceptance letter at HYD check-in.

🧭 Journey timeline

Tap a step to mark it done. Progress is saved on this phone.

    🚇 Nagoya Station → dorm by subway

    Fare 430–470 JPY either way. Tap 🚇 Metro on the map to see both routes drawn in the real line colours. (With the two big bags on move-in day, the taxi below is easier — the subway is your everyday route.)

    RED Plan A — Sakura-dōri Line

    1. At Nagoya Station find the Sakura-dōri Line (red). Board the train toward Tokushige (徳重).
    2. Ride ~5 min, get off at Hisaya-ōdōri (久屋大通).
    3. Change to the Meijō Line (purple) — clockwise (右回り), i.e. for Nagoya Dome-mae Yada.
    4. Ride ~15 min, get off at Nagoya Dome-mae Yada (ナゴヤドーム前矢田).

    YELLOW Plan B — Higashiyama Line

    1. At Nagoya Station find the Higashiyama Line (yellow). Board and ride ~5 min.
    2. Get off at Sakae (栄).
    3. Change to the Meijō Line (purple) — clockwise (右回り), i.e. for Nagoya Dome-mae Yada.
    4. Ride ~12 min, get off at Nagoya Dome-mae Yada (ナゴヤドーム前矢田).

    WALK Station → International Residence Daiko (~3 min)

    1. Take the elevator up to Entrance 1 (1番出口).
    2. Turn right at the corner just outside the exit.
    3. Walk straight ~3 min — International Residence Daiko is on your right.
