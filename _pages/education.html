Ph.D. in Intelligent Systems
Nagoya University, Graduate School of Informatics

I am a PhD scholar at the Fujii Laboratory, Department of Intelligent Systems, Graduate School of Informatics. My research focuses on computer vision for human motion: unsupervised temporal segmentation of continuous movement, pose-based action recognition, and measurement-grade sports analytics that state their own limits.

M.Sc. in Physics July 2023 to May 2025
Indian Institute of Technology Gandhinagar

During my master's I moved from physics into computer vision research, working on unsupervised temporal segmentation of sports actions with graph neural networks. That work led to papers at the IEEE STAR workshop and the IEEE ICIP workshops, a published Indian patent, and benchmark datasets for javelin, diving and boxing.

B.Sc. in Physics June 2019 to July 2022
Berhampur University

I studied physics at Khemundi Degree College under Berhampur University, where I ranked first in my department. Alongside core physics and mathematics, my bachelor's is where my interest in coding and programming took root: writing small numerical programs to simulate the systems I was studying showed me how well computation and physics fit together, and that mix of physical intuition and code is what later carried me into computer science and computer vision research.
